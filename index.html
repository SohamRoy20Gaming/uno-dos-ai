How to Play DOS

Objective

Be the first player to empty your hand. In 200-pt Match mode, the round winner scores the point value of cards left in the opponent's hand until someone reaches 200 points.

Matching the Center Row

  • The Center Row starts with 2 piles. You can match one or multiple Center Row piles on your turn (each pile at most once).
  • Single Match: Play 1 card matching the number of a Center Row card (any color).
  • Double Match: Play 2 cards whose numbers add up to the Center Row card's number (e.g., 3 + 4 = 7).
  • If you can't match: Click the Draw Pile to draw 1 card. You may immediately match with it, or click End Turn.

Color Match Bonuses

  • Single Color Match: Your played card also matches the Center card's color → at the end of your turn, place 1 extra card from your hand into the Center Row!
  • Double Color Match: Both cards in a Double Match match the Center card's color → place 1 extra card into the Center Row AND the Bot draws 1 penalty card!

Special Wild Cards

  • Wild # (Colored): Counts as any number 1–10 in its color (20 pts).
  • Wild DOS (Rainbow 2): Counts as the number 2 in any color, triggering Color Match Bonuses! (50 pts).

Calling "DOS!"

When you are down to 2 cards, press the glowing DOS! button before ending your turn—or the Bot can catch you for +2 penalty cards! Watch closely: if the Bot forgets to call DOS, hit Catch Bot!
